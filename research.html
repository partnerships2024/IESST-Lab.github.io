Research

Seven objectives that take energy systems from monitoring to autonomous optimisation.

Seven research objectives

Objective 1

Renewable Energy Forecasting

AI/ML models for solar PV generation, wind generation, renewable-energy availability and short-term energy production.

Objective 2

Energy Demand Forecasting

Predictive models using historical consumption, weather, time, seasonal patterns and occupancy/usage information where available.

Objective 3

Intelligent Battery Management

AI-driven state-of-charge and state-of-health estimation, charging/discharging optimisation, degradation-aware scheduling and storage utilisation.

Objective 4

Intelligent Microgrid Optimisation

Algorithms coordinating solar, wind, battery, grid and loads under operational constraints and energy costs.

Objective 5

AI-Based Intelligent Control

Reinforcement learning and other adaptive control approaches for real-time energy management.

Objective 6

Energy Digital Twin

A computational twin for monitoring, simulation, prediction, optimisation, scenario analysis and decision support.

Objective 7

Industrial Demonstration

A prototype energy-management platform evaluated under realistic operating scenarios.

Renewable-energy and demand forecasting

Accurate forecasting is critical for intelligent energy management.

Solar inputs

  • Solar irradiance
  • Temperature
  • Humidity
  • Cloud conditions
  • Historical PV output
  • Time of day
  • Seasonal information

Wind inputs

  • Wind speed
  • Wind direction
  • Temperature
  • Pressure
  • Historical generation
  • Weather forecasts

Demand inputs

  • Historical demand
  • Weather
  • Time
  • Seasonal patterns

Artificial intelligence framework

Machine learning

  • Energy forecasting
  • Fault detection
  • Battery health prediction
  • Energy-consumption modelling
  • Renewable-resource assessment

Deep learning

Nonlinear relationships between weather and renewable generation, and between consumption patterns and energy demand.

Reinforcement learning

An agent observes generation, demand, battery state, price, grid and environment, then chooses to charge, discharge, use renewables, import, export or adjust controllable loads.

Battery energy storage intelligence

State of Charge

How much energy is currently stored?

State of Health

How healthy is the battery?

Remaining useful life

How long can the battery keep operating effectively?

Optimal charging and discharging

When should the battery charge, and when should stored energy be released?

The system balances energy availability, demand, battery health, cost and renewable utilisation.

Intelligent energy optimisation

Minimise energy cost and losses while maximising renewable-energy utilisation and maintaining system reliability.

  • Mathematical optimisation
  • Mixed-integer optimisation
  • Evolutionary algorithms
  • Model predictive control
  • Reinforcement learning
  • Hybrid AI-optimisation methods

Energy digital twin

Researchers and industrial partners can test energy-management strategies without affecting the physical system.

  • What-if analysis
  • Renewable-generation scenarios
  • Battery scheduling
  • Demand-response scenarios
  • Fault simulation
  • Energy-efficiency analysis
  • Optimisation before deployment
