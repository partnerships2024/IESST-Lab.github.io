Roadmap

From modelling and AI in year one to industrial translation in year three.

Research, prototype, validate, demonstrate, translate

Seven phases move the work from requirements to industrial demonstration.

  1. Industrial Requirements

    Target application, system configuration, constraints, data and performance requirements, industrial KPIs.

  2. Mathematical & Computational Modelling

    Models for renewable generation, demand, batteries, microgrid operation and energy flows.

  3. AI Development

    Forecasting, anomaly-detection, battery-intelligence, optimisation and control algorithms.

  4. Digital Twin

    The computational representation of the energy system.

  5. Prototype Development

    Sensors, IoT, edge computing, AI, energy management and visualisation.

  6. Validation

    Simulations and controlled experiments.

  7. Industrial Demonstration

    Evaluation under realistic operating conditions.

Three-year roadmap

Year 1

Modelling & AI

  • Energy-system modelling
  • Renewable-energy datasets
  • PV/wind forecasting
  • Demand forecasting
  • Battery modelling
  • Initial AI models
Year 2

Intelligent Energy Platform

  • Energy digital twin
  • Reinforcement learning
  • Microgrid optimisation
  • Edge AI
  • Intelligent battery management
  • Prototype development
Year 3

Demonstration & Industrial Translation

  • Integrated prototype
  • Hardware-in-the-loop testing
  • Field-oriented demonstration
  • Performance evaluation
  • Industrial partnership
  • IP and technology-transfer assessment
  • Commercialisation strategy

Long-term impact

The aim is to establish ICRI-STE as a research and innovation contributor in AI-driven sustainable energy systems, with a portfolio spanning renewable energy, energy storage, smart grids, microgrids, AI, digital twins, intelligent control and sustainable energy management. That makes this a scalable research programme rather than a single project.
