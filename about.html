About the lab

Intelligent Energy Systems & Sustainable Technologies Lab, part of International Council for Research & Innovation in Science, Technology & Engineering (ICRI-STE).

Executive summary

The global transition to sustainable energy needs technologies that integrate renewable generation, storage, variable demand and distributed energy resources efficiently and reliably. Solar and wind are inherently variable, and conventional energy management often lacks the adaptability that decentralised systems need.

This programme develops an AI-enabled renewable-energy and smart-microgrid platform that combines solar PV, wind, battery storage, IoT sensing, edge computing, machine learning, optimisation, intelligent control and energy digital twins.

The system forecasts generation and demand, optimises storage and power flows, detects abnormal operating conditions and coordinates distributed energy resources. It is a flagship industrial R&D programme of the IESST Lab, built as a foundation for work with renewable-energy companies, utilities, technology providers, smart-grid organisations and industrial energy users.

The industrial problem

  • Increasing integration of intermittent renewable energy
  • Uncertainty in solar and wind generation
  • Rapidly changing electricity demand
  • Limited and costly energy-storage capacity
  • Energy losses and inefficient power management
  • Increasing complexity of distributed energy resources
  • Need for reliable microgrid operation
  • Difficulty predicting renewable generation accurately
  • Need for intelligent demand-response mechanisms
  • Increasing requirements for sustainable and low-carbon energy systems

Traditional energy-management systems rely on predefined rules and conventional optimisation. They may not respond efficiently to rapidly changing operating conditions.

How can AI, computational modelling, optimisation, and intelligent control be integrated to create an adaptive energy-management system capable of optimally coordinating renewable generation, energy storage, demand, and grid interaction in real time?

Vision and aim

We aim to move from conventional energy management toward autonomous intelligent energy management: from generate, distribute, consume to sense, predict, optimise, control, learn and adapt.

  1. Sense
  2. Predict
  3. Optimise
  4. Control
  5. Learn
  6. Adapt
To develop and validate an AI-enabled intelligent renewable-energy and smart-microgrid platform capable of real-time forecasting, optimisation, energy-storage management, and intelligent control for sustainable energy systems.

Sustainability impact

The project supports sustainable-energy goals through renewable integration, efficiency and reduced carbon intensity.

  • Increase renewable-energy utilisation
  • Improve energy efficiency
  • Optimise energy storage
  • Reduce avoidable energy losses
  • Support distributed renewable generation
  • Improve energy-system resilience
  • Enable data-driven sustainable energy management
