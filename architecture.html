Architecture

Generation, storage, sensing, edge AI, intelligence, digital twin and energy management in one stack.

Seven connected layers

Select a layer to see what it does.

Layer 1: Renewable Energy Generation

Solar PV, wind energy and hybrid renewable generation.

Layer 2: Energy Storage

Battery energy storage, battery-management system and charging/discharging controller.

Layer 3: IoT & Smart Sensing

Voltage, current, power, generation, battery status, temperature, demand and environmental conditions.

Layer 4: Edge AI

Real-time monitoring, anomaly detection, rapid prediction, low-latency control and reduced communication needs.

Layer 5: AI Intelligence

Renewable and demand forecasting, battery prediction, anomaly detection, optimisation and intelligent control.

Layer 6: Energy Digital Twin

A continuously updated computational representation of the physical energy system.

Layer 7: Intelligent Energy Management

Optimal generation utilisation, battery charging/discharging, grid interaction, demand response and energy allocation.

System architecture

A typical renewable/BESS architecture includes renewable generation, power conversion, battery storage, a battery-management system, the grid and an energy-management system.

Digital twin loop

The twin keeps a computational representation of the physical microgrid in step with live sensor data, so AI prediction and optimisation can be run and checked virtually before any control action reaches the real system.
